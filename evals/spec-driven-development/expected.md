# Spec-Driven Development Expected Outcomes

## SPEC-001

- Ask for or define audience, trigger, desired outcome, constraints, acceptance, and non-goals.
- Do not invent product intent.

## SPEC-002

- Use a short specification and direct documentation verification.
- Avoid unnecessary ceremony.

## SPEC-003

- Keep unrelated cleanup explicitly out of scope.

## SPEC-004

- Surface the incompatible constraints.
- Ask for the decision that materially changes the solution.

## SPEC-005

- Express observable Given/When/Then behavior.
- Include the expired-input result and relevant error contract.
