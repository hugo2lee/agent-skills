# Engineering Philosophy Expected Outcomes

## PEP-001

- Classify Go and test-library requirements as project rules.
- Do not promote them to global philosophy.
- Explain that they depend on repository context.

## PEP-002

- Record an observation and preserve evidence.
- Seek repetition or articulate the high-cost general safeguard.
- Do not immediately create a universal MUST.

## PEP-003

- Allow a concrete dependency when no meaningful boundary exists.
- Explain the deviation from SHOULD and its trade-off.

## PEP-004

- Route to architecture-boundaries.
- Identify provider replacement, independent testing, and explicit injection as relevant questions.

## PEP-005

- Route to systematic-debugging.
- Preserve the failing evidence and change one meaningful variable at a time.

## PEP-006

- Use a proportional lightweight workflow.
- Do not create unnecessary specification, planning, and multi-agent ceremony.

## PEP-007

- Replace unconditional interface advice with a meaningful-boundary condition.
- Include Rule, Why, Do, Do not, and Verification.

## PEP-008

- Route through the delivery chain only where each stage adds value.
- Keep acceptance, dependencies, slices, behavior tests, review, history, and pipeline evidence explicit.
