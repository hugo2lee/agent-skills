# Planning and Task Breakdown Expected Outcomes

## PLAN-001

- Split work by behavior or subsystem.
- State dependencies, risks, checkpoints, and verification.

## PLAN-002

- Put discovery and safety-net tests before storage changes.
- Delay old-path removal until behavior is proven.

## PLAN-003

- Do not parallelize overlapping writes.
- Split ownership or keep the decision on one work path.

## PLAN-004

- Keep the package-wide rename out of scope unless approved.

## PLAN-005

- Add completion criteria and evidence to every meaningful task.
